## Outcome and scope rules

- **Game writes.** The admitted world uses `GameTransaction.run_gameplay` or `run_guarded_action`. Garden-owned state uses G. Local class, harvest and expedition actions that affect both participants use G+P. Guest actions update the host's G projection, then the guest separately writes its own P. The live lease and native authorization cover the relevant participants. Raw or legacy serializer entry points refuse opted-in world writes. A verified abort restores snapshots. An uncertain result retains evidence and blocks replay. A confirmed cleanup-pending action retains its committed state and warning. Profile-dependent feedback waits for the guest acknowledgment. [Transaction](../game_transaction.gd#L129) · [Publication](../game.gd#L1310) · [Profile scopes](../game.gd#L1422)
- **Title management.** No play-session lease is held while changing a slot. The guarded management boundary locks the root and refuses conflicting leases, stale confirmation digests, and pending or ambiguous evidence. It reports completion separately from callback execution. Replacement can hold a verified new Garden while keeping Continue disabled pending cleanup. Deletion success requires a confirmed outcome. Archive copies are retained for review even if canonical compensation restores the original bytes. [Title replacement](../main_menu.gd#L635) · [Title deletion](../main_menu.gd#L733) · [Management outcomes](../persistence_operations.gd#L314)
- **Lease and metadata writes.** Short root authorization covers lease marker and selection updates. There is no long-lived native handle for a play session. A stale or foreign owner cannot renew or release another owner's marker. Read-only slot review does not mutate save bytes. [Session owner](../guarded_title_session.gd#L175) · [Marker update](../save_slots.gd#L533)
- **Recovery.** The title presents the actual owning record discovered from canonical and metadata seeds. B2 revalidates the selected participant set and lease state before mutation. It restores verified originals or cleans verified committed evidence. Unsupported or contradictory evidence remains blocked. [Title review](../main_menu.gd#L424) · [B2 executor](../persistence_operations.gd#L452)
