# Gate portals and Garden interaction cleanup

Garden gates now display a blue, glowing animated spiral continuously. The onward
Wilds gate displays the same effect only when the primary enemy and every scout are
defeated. New encounters hide it again. The effect follows the generated gate location
and adds a faint blue local light. Final Heart arenas do not expose an onward portal.

Garden roots now use three uneven, tapered strands with small lateral forks and bark
normal detail instead of one uniform cord. Active beds reserve space from forest trees
and pond shorelines. Pond placement searches for a clear position as the Garden grows;
flowers and grass follow the resulting pond reservations too.

E at the Heart opens Knowledge, Classes and Garden care. Garden care has Tending,
Seed reclamation and Monster research pages. Reclamation and offerings moved from the
old separate scenery objects to the Heart, retaining their research requirements.
Obsolete standalone service models are hidden. E at the Altar opens the existing
identification/sacrifice interface; E at the Gate opens preparation with destination
selection. Direct plot cultivation remains at the planting beds.

Removed old floating Heart action lists and retired Garden shortcuts for research,
donation, affinity/class cycling, expansion, offshoot decisions, awakening, experiment,
route and weapon-slot cycling. These actions now use their station menus. The seed
progression tree is a viewer; the older duplicate breeding controls are hidden in favor
of the drag-and-drop Altar. Wilds combat and checkpoint controls remain intact.

Validation: portal locks with primary/scout survivors; centralized service access and
retired station rejection; all nine beds checked against trees and pond footprints at
eleven Garden sizes; existing Heart, cultivation, Altar, combat, run-flow and full solo
checks. Rendered Garden portal, cleared Wilds portal and Garden care menu reviewed.
Only the latest playable EXE, Mystical Garden v0.2.exe, is replaced.
