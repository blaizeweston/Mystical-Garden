# Core skills and enemy responses

The ranking comes from the authored moves and effects. The priority is how much of an enemy’s job a skill can shut down, how many builds can repeat that interaction, and whether the enemy can answer it with a readable move. Live expeditions will establish frequency and balance.

## Skill categories

A skill can belong to several categories. Learn from an actual outcome, such as a canceled rally or two enemies damaged by one pulse. The same host-world observation can cover several skills and weapon variants.

| Interaction | Core skills | What the enemy experiences |
|---|---|---|
| Stop an approach or lane change | Spore Seal, Moon Snare, Constellation, Rime Bloom, Phantom Step, Worldbloom | Roots hold an enemy in place. Trap crossings, roots on impact and beams provide different opportunities for pursuers to get caught. Dream Lure roots enemies when the decoy bursts. Thorn Line deals repeated damage along a crossing. Some cards add roots to otherwise different effects. |
| Disrupt an attack | Hush, Moon Snare, Lantern Burst | Hush cancels a committed lesser-enemy windup. Moon Snare roots entrants after it arms and deals bonus damage to foes in a windup. Lantern Burst staggers eligible enemies between attacks. These are distinct outcomes. |
| Move enemies or break a formation | Gravity Well, Riptide, Sun Pillar, Eclipse Maw | Pulls concentrate enemies or draw escorts out of position. Pushes break contact or separate an escort from support. Melee Gravity Well centers on the caster. Riptide’s outgoing wave pulls with melee weapons and pushes with ranged weapons. Its return restores health along the same path. The displacement phases affect eligible idle enemies. |
| Damage a clustered group or deny ground | Ember Wake, Mirror Bloom, Sun Pillar, Eclipse Maw, Blood Siphon, Grave Lantern, Dream Lure | Timed fields reward keeping enemies grouped. Mirror Bloom hits two opposed positions around the caster. Grave Lantern uses a nearby death as the trigger. Brood seedlings are especially vulnerable to these pulses. |
| Punish a line, nearby chain, or priority target | Seed Lance, Chain Spark, Whisper Mark, Crownlink, Rime Bloom | Seed Lance rewards alignment. Chain Spark rewards neighbors in jump range. Marks, links and Brittle create a follow-up opportunity, including for teammates. |
| Survive contact or restore the frontline | Dewfall, Ironbark, Worldbloom, Riptide, Blood Siphon | Heal, cleanse, and ward effects improve a party’s position or sustain. These provide a combat advantage without being direct evidence of an enemy action being canceled. |
| Change the target or escape its pressure | Phantom Step, Dream Lure | A closing step roots along the final stretch of a dash. A ranged backstep roots a strip across the seed landing. The decoy can draw pursuing enemies or redirect their aim while active. Track the resulting miss or diversion if this category later needs a response. |

## Visibility in combat

The ground warning shows where a move can land. An actual release leaves a short matching sweep, strike or path. A canceled learned charge shows **Stopped**. A blocked Scattered Hatch marks the occupied site. Pending webs, spores and embers are named at the site and switch to distinct active patterns.

**Slowed**, **Rooted**, **Hastened**, **Rallied**, **Sheltered** and **Carrying trophy** attach to the affected player or monster when the effect takes hold. Pulls, shelter breaks and thefts have brief callouts. Spare Tether shows the health actually restored. Applied status callouts follow the rendered actor and clear when the effect ends or the encounter resets.

## Learned responses

- **Cast denial.** A confirmed cancellation of an eligible committed attack contributes when the caster survives. Hush, eligible heavy interruptions and weapon suppression feed the same record. Support rites still require a viable hatch, corpse or escort where the move depends on one. From floor 7, eligible Shepherds may follow a canceled rally with one 1.15-second Relay order. The tether and flank are visible. Rooting or defeating the selected escort, or breaking its route or line to the caster, can stop it.
- **Area pressure.** A Core contact phase contributes when it damages at least two enemies or kills a genuine Broodmother seedling. From floor 10, eligible Broodmothers can use two safe sites 6.7 m apart. Both sites are marked before release. Standing at a marked site prevents that hatch. The ordinary summon budget and reward rules still apply.

- **Bellcap Cantor, Echo Toll.** From floor 7, a canceled chant can lead to a smaller haste circle centered on a marked escort. It can reach two allies, including escorts outside the original chant. Kill or displace the marked escort, break its line to the Cantor, or interrupt the rite to stop the toll.
- **Pallbearer Bloom, Spare Tether.** From floor 8, a severed corpse tether can lead to a different corpse. A successful cast consumes that corpse for one point of healing and can haste one marked escort. Break the new corpse tether or interrupt the rite to deny the heal.
- **Root Choir, Antiphon.** From floor 9, an interrupted sweep can be followed by one smaller sweep aimed from a marked escort. The sweep deals one damage and briefly slows. Step outside the sector, kill or displace the escort, break its line to the Choir, or interrupt the rite.

- **Route denial.** A new root on an engaged, non-winding pursuer with distance still to cover contributes once per actor. A root that only extends existing control provides no new evidence. The learned route still needs an open path at impact, and a fresh root or live Thorn Line can stop it.
- **Shelter break.** A Core displacement must move a real ally out of an active Grazer canopy. The Grazer records that escort's spawn token and can rejoin it only if the warned destination still shelters it.

A cleared floor contributes each observed tactic once, regardless of cast count or party size. The existing host-world memory stores the scores and quiet-floor decay. At score 6, a deterministic roll can select a response. Each eligible monster gets one new response per encounter. The existing warnings carry the plan to guests. The diagnostics log the confirmed outcome and the response ID for later tuning.
